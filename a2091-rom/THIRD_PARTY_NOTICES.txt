Copperline A2091/A590 open ROM - source inventory and notices
================================================================

The ROM and disk-loadable scsi.device are built from:

1. New Copperline A2091 loader, WD33C93/DMAC glue, interrupt bridge, and image
   builder under the BSD 2-Clause license in LICENSE.
2. Selected source files from A4091 software v42.39, commit
   ce6d62cf2db5c6bf990e4d675d633f2dedcc84a6:
   https://github.com/A4091/a4091-software
3. The A4091 mounter submodule at commit
   48c8c1cfdcda37fb9d4d6fd7b1bfd8f789402c1a:
   https://github.com/A4091/mounter
4. The 68000 ZX0 decompressor from Salvador at commit
   bf562ffec873a72ee324beb4358fa680121e5852:
   https://github.com/emmanuel-marty/salvador

No Commodore ROM bytes, Linux driver code, or AROS driver code are included.
The source files retain their per-file notices. The notices relevant to the
binary are reproduced below.
