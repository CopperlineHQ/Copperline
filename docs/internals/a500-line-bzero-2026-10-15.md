## Observer correction found during review

The earlier kinds 25–27 use BLTCPT=$11000 and BLTDPT=$10000. In line
mode, later D writes follow the updated C pointer. Their OR scan of
$10000–$107fe and words $10000/$10050/$100a0 therefore observe the initial
D region, not all later line writes. Those checked words match across the
captures, but this does not establish that every generated line pixel is
zero or that all later destination words agree. The kind-28 observer must
scan both regions and sample $11050/$110a0 before flag semantics are inferred.
